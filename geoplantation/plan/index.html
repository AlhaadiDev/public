Plan proposal

One map for six months

Lite is a real morning on one GeoPDF, including a saved offline map. The paid plan starts when the work outgrows that one file.

One file Lite holds a single GeoPDF. A second file is Premium.
Offline stays A saved satellite map is included on Lite, Premium, and Enterprise.
Six months The trial ends. After that, Premium or Enterprise.

6-month trial

Lite

Try one farm. One GeoPDF, map tools, an offline map, and up to 30 scouting reports.

Recommended

Premium

The plan to sell. More GeoPDFs, the estate dashboard, and sharing a map file.

Contract

Enterprise

The company plan. Custom limits, more plantations, and team scouting when that is built.

On Lite

The visit still works

  • The signal drops. Yesterday’s satellite area is already on the phone.
  • They open their one GeoPDF, draw the block, drop a pin, and add a photo.
  • They write a scouting note. Up to 30 reports stay on the phone.
  • After six months, this trial ends.

Why they pay

The work gets bigger

  • A second block arrives as another GeoPDF. Lite stops at one file.
  • A manager asks for the estate dashboard. That is Premium.
  • The office needs the map file sent across. Sharing is Premium.
  • A company with many estates is Enterprise, on a contract.

If someone asks about drone, sensor, crops, or pest

Those screens are in the app, and they sit on Premium and Enterprise as reference. The data is still sample or fixed, so they are not part of the Lite trial and they are not the reason to buy. Shop is also excluded from Lite. Team sharing for scouting is not built yet.

Decisions to confirm

  1. Lite lasts six months, then the user needs Premium or Enterprise.
  2. The paid step up is a second GeoPDF, the dashboard, and sharing. Offline maps stay on Lite.
  3. Enterprise is a contract, not a longer feature list that is still unbuilt.
