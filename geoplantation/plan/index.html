Reference screens

Drone, sensor, crops, pest, and shop use sample or fixed data. They are on Premium and Enterprise only. They are not the reason to buy. Team scouting is not built yet.

Decisions

  1. Lite ends after six months. Then Premium or Enterprise.
  2. The paid step up is a second GeoPDF, the dashboard, sharing, and scouting. Offline maps stay on Lite.
  3. Enterprise is a contract, not a list of features that are still unbuilt.
